🎮 How The Warp Button Works

1. The Objective: Be the last person to press the button before the countdown clock hits 00:00.


2. Cost & Timing: Each press costs 0.0001 ETH (~$0.30 on Base) and adds +30 seconds to the clock (capped at 30 mins).


3. Sudden Death: When remaining time is under 60 seconds, every click resets the timer to exactly 60s for maximum tension.


4. Pot Distribution:

  • 🏆 80% sent directly to the last clicker (Winner).
