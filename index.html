How Warp V2 works

Be last. Press before the clock ends. The last player wins the full displayed prize pot.

Ticket and timer. The exact ticket is read from the game on Base; wallet gas is extra. A round starts with 10 minutes. Each press adds 30 seconds, capped at 30 minutes remaining. Under 60 seconds, a press resets the timer to 60 seconds.

Every ticket: 85% joins the prize pot, 10% seeds the next round, 5% becomes the creator's claimable fee. External ETH deposits split 50% to the pot and 50% to the creator's claimable balance.

Settlement and claims. Anyone can settle an expired round; the next press also settles it. The winner receives a claimable balance and chooses when to withdraw. A transaction is required: the timer cannot send one by itself.

Token rewards. WETH fees from Clanker LP are forwarded to the prize pot and creator via the verified fee router. Non-WETH token rewards are held in custody.

Before you play. Tickets are paid entries and are not returned when another player wins. A countdown is an estimate from the latest verified block; the contract decides which press arrives in time.
